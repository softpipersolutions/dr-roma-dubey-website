# Imagery — Dr. Roma Dubey Design System

**Approach:** soft, real, warm-graded photography — women, mothers, families, and calm clinical moments. Natural light, warm skin tones, shallow depth of field. Photos sit in **rounded frames** (`--radius-lg`/`xl`), often over a blush/clay blob or cream panel for depth. A subtle warm "protection gradient" (espresso → transparent) is laid over photos when text sits on top.

## Photo treatment rules
- Warm grade: nudge toward warm whites; avoid cold blue casts.
- Rounded corners always (`--radius-lg` min); never hard 90° photo corners.
- Pair a photo with ONE decorative blob, not many.
- Over-photo text: espresso protection gradient + white text; keep contrast AA.
